# KB Synthesis — Multi-Note Blog Posts & External Outputs

When the user asks to synthesize multiple KB notes into a blog post, article, or Notion page:

## Workflow

1. **Read all relevant notes in full** — every note the user identifies. Do not sample or skim.
2. **Identify cross-cutting themes** — what tensions, contrasts, or complementary perspectives emerge across the sources?
3. **Structure with numbered parts** — clear sections with descriptive headers. A typical blog post structure:
   - Hook/introduction framing the question
   - Part 1: The official/majority view (most authoritative source first)
   - Part 2: The critique/counter-evidence
   - Part 3: Alternative perspectives or root causes
   - Part 4 (optional): Unexpected angles or historical context
   - Synthesis: table or summary tying sources together
   - What it means / conclusion
   - Sources list
4. **Write in the user's language** (French in this case, content in source language)
5. **Bold key claims and statistics** for scanability
6. **Use tables** for comparison/synthesis sections
7. **Present draft in chat first** — let user review before posting to external platform
8. **Don't pre-search the destination platform** — user will tell you where to post when ready

## Tone

- Analytical, not advocacy. Present evidence from each source at its own confidence level.
- Let contradictions stand — don't resolve them artificially.
- The KB notes are the primary sources. The synthesis adds structure and connection, not new claims.
